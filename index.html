2. Information we process

Depending on the App's configuration and how you use it, we and our service providers may process:

  • Push and device identifiers: Apple push notification token, OneSignal subscription/user identifiers and device or app identifiers.
  • Technical information: IP address, device model, operating system version, language, time zone, network information and app version.
  • Usage and notification information: app session or interaction events, notification delivery/open status and notification preferences.
  • Communications: information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

IP addresses may allow approximate location to be inferred. We do not request precise GPS location for the notification functionality described here.

The exact data collected depends on the SDK settings and any other services included in the released build. We will update this notice before introducing materially different data processing.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use information to operate the App, deliver requested push notifications, manage notification preferences, maintain security, troubleshoot errors and respond to support requests. Where applicable under GDPR/UK GDPR, our legal bases may include your consent for optional notifications or related processing, our legitimate interests in maintaining a secure and functional service, performance of a contract where necessary, and compliance with legal obligations.

Where consent is required, we seek it before the relevant processing and you can withdraw it. The iOS permission to display notifications is separate from any other consent that applicable law may require.

4. OneSignal push notifications

We use OneSignal to support push notifications. OneSignal may process push tokens, device and subscription identifiers, IP addresses, device characteristics, notification activity and app interaction data on our behalf, depending on configuration.

You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Panda Slot. Turning off notifications may not by itself erase data previously collected. To request deletion, contact us using the details below.

Read OneSignal's Privacy Policy.
